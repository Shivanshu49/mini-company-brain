SOURCE_TYPE: document (RFC)
ID: RFC-014
TITLE: Refund retry strategy for refund-service
AUTHOR: Rahul Verma
APPROVED_BY: Priya Sharma
DATE: 2026-08-14
STATUS: Approved
RELATED: PAY-231, MTG-2026-08-12 (Payments Weekly Sync)

Context:
PAY-231 reported that about 4% of refunds fail when the Kosha Bank refund API times out (30 second timeout, see DOC-006). refund-service previously retried immediately up to 2 times. Immediate retries hit the Kosha Bank rate limit (HTTP 429) and made failures worse.

Decision:
refund-service will retry failed refund calls using exponential backoff with jitter.
- Base delay: 2 seconds
- Multiplier: 2
- Maximum retries: 3
- After 3 failed retries the refund is marked FAILED and the merchant is notified.

This decision was made by Priya Sharma in the Payments Weekly Sync on 2026-08-12 (MTG-2026-08-12).

Idempotency:
Every refund request sent to Kosha Bank carries refund_id as the idempotency key, so Kosha Bank will not execute the same refund twice.

Out of scope:
Changes to ledger-service are out of scope for RFC-014.

Rollout:
Behind feature flag refund_backoff_v2, ramped from 10% to 100%.
