TITLE: PayNest company overview
AUTHOR: Karan Mehta
DATE: 2026-06-02

PayNest is a Noida-based fintech that provides UPI and card payment acceptance for small and mid-sized merchants across India. PayNest processes payments, refunds, and T+1 settlements for about 18,000 merchants.

The Payments team is led by Priya Sharma (Engineering Manager, Payments). Team members:
- Rahul Verma: Backend Engineer, owner of refund-service
- Neha Gupta: Backend Engineer, owner of settlement-service and ledger-service
- Ananya Iyer: Site Reliability Engineer, leads the on-call rotation
- Arjun Rao: Risk and Compliance Engineer, owner of fraud-guard
- Karan Mehta: Product Manager, Payments

Core services:
- refund-service: processes merchant-initiated refunds through the Kosha Bank refund API.
- settlement-service: pays out merchant balances on a T+1 schedule.
- ledger-service: double-entry ledger that records every payment, refund, and payout.
- fraud-guard: rule engine that screens payouts and can place them on hold.

Kosha Bank is PayNest's sponsor bank and UPI partner. All refunds and payouts go through Kosha Bank APIs.
