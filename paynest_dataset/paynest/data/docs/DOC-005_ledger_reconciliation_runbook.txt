TITLE: ledger-service reconciliation runbook
AUTHOR: Neha Gupta
DATE: 2026-07-15

The ledger-recon job runs nightly at 02:00 IST. It compares every entry in ledger-service against the Kosha Bank daily statement.

When a mismatch is found, ledger-recon automatically creates a ticket assigned to the ledger-service owner (Neha Gupta).

Common causes of mismatches:
- Duplicate ledger entries for the same refund or payout
- Payouts held by fraud-guard but recorded as sent
- Late statement files from Kosha Bank

Manual fix: never delete ledger entries. Post a reversing entry with reason code RECON_FIX and link the ticket ID.
