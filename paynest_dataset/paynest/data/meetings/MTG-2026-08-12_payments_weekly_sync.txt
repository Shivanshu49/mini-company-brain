1. PAY-231: refund failures
Rahul Verma showed that immediate retries in refund-service cause HTTP 429 rate-limit errors from Kosha Bank, which increases refund failures.
Options discussed:
a) Fixed delay between retries
b) Exponential backoff with jitter
c) Queue-based retries with a dead-letter queue

Decision:
Priya Sharma decided on option (b), exponential backoff with jitter and a maximum of 3 retries, because it respects Kosha Bank rate limits and keeps refunds fast for merchants. Option (c) was rejected for now because it needs about 3 weeks of work.
