1. RBI audit observation
An RBI audit observation from June flagged payouts to possible mule accounts that showed sudden volume spikes.

2. Proposal: fraud-guard rule FG-R9
Arjun Rao proposed FG-R9: hold a payout when a merchant's daily volume exceeds 300% of its 7-day average.
Karan Mehta raised a concern that festive sales could cause false positives for genuine merchants.
Neha Gupta asked that settlement-service show the hold reason to merchants.

Decision:
Priya Sharma approved FG-R9 to go live on 2026-08-01. The false positive rate will be reviewed after two weeks.

Action items:
- Arjun Rao: implement FG-R9 in fraud-guard and document it in DOC-003.
- Neha Gupta: show fraud-guard hold reasons in settlement-service merchant notifications.
